How Retake works

Describe a demo of your app in a sentence. Retake drives a real browser through it and hands you the video. When the app changes, you re-run — nobody re-records anything by hand.

1

You say it

Record a demo of my app at localhost:3000 showing the sign-up flow.

To the agent you already use. It reads your app, writes the steps, checks every one against the live page, and only then records.

→
2

You watch it

demo.mp4a still for every scenea written record of what happened

It lands in this window, newest on top. Download for the file, Show in folder for the rest.

→
3

You change your mind

No zooms. Hide the cursor. Square for a post.

In plain words, and most of it comes back in seconds — the recording is already made. Say it once and Retake keeps it for the project.

↺ And when your app changes, you run the same file again. That is the whole idea: the demo outlives the video.

When you want to know it is right

A recording is finished when it records. Everything below is optional, run afterwards on a take you already have, and none of it changes the video.

Ask your agent in plain words. It has all of these.

  • Does it look right? Write what should be visible on a scene — “the board shows two items” — and every later recording answers it.
  • What did nobody think to check? Every frame gets read for the ten ways a screen goes wrong: text cut off, things overlapping, writing you cannot read, a spinner still spinning, a dev badge left in shot.
  • Does it add up? What the demo typed against what came back. It found a quote showing one price for two flights that way.
  • What happens off the happy path? The button pressed twice, the page reloaded mid-flight, the app with nothing in it yet.
  • Did that get fixed? Say something is wrong once and every later take answers it, with the six seconds of video that show it.

Tell it what your product is for and these get much better — otherwise they judge your app as a generic web page and anything you did on purpose can come back as a mistake. Say it once; it is kept.

They report, they do not block. On Retake's own help page the frame check found eight of eight faults deliberately planted in it and complained about nothing on a clean recording, and that number is a floor rather than a promise.

Where things live

Each demo is a small YAML file in demos/ — that file is the demo. Videos land in outputs/<name>/: demo.mp4, stills/, and proof-log.md saying exactly what happened, step by step.

Good to know

  • Two kinds of take. A demo proves how something works — a client walkthrough, a clip for a pull request, a lesson — and keeps everything but your product out of the frame. That is the default. A launch video presents your product in public, and only then do a title card, music and a closing card belong. Your agent asks where it is going; if you shrug, it makes a demo.
  • Retake never starts your app without you saying so.
  • Apps behind a login: when the agent needs a sign-in, a form appears here asking for a demo account. What you type is written to one local file (.env in your workspace) and never sent to the agent or anywhere else. The sign-in runs before the camera and is cut off; the session is kept, so later takes skip it. For codes from your phone, SSO or a captcha: retake signin demos/<name>.yaml — log in once by hand, Retake keeps the session.
  • A failed step names what was on screen at the time — read the proof log before guessing.
