How Retake works

Describe a demo of your app in a sentence. Retake drives a real browser through it and hands you the video. When the app changes, you re-run — nobody re-records anything by hand.

1 · Ask for a demo

Talk to the agent you already use — Claude Code or Codex. It has Retake's tools.

Record a demo of my app at localhost:3000 showing the sign-up flow.
What demos are worth recording for this project? Record the best three.

The agent reads your app, writes the steps, checks every one of them against the live page, and only then records. You can watch it work on this window's front page.

2 · Judge the take

The video lands here — newest on top, one still per scene under it. Download for the file, Show in folder for everything else — the stills, the proof log, the manifest.

3 · Direct it like a videographer

Say what you want changed, in plain words:

  • “No zooms — keep the camera still.”
  • “Hide the cursor.” · “Shorter captions.” · “No captions.”
  • “Hold the ending longer.” · “Cut the first two seconds.”
  • “Make it square for a post.” · “Vertical for a reel.”

Say it once — Retake remembers it for the project. Most changes re-render in seconds without re-recording.

Where things live

Each demo is a small YAML file in demos/ — that file is the demo. Videos land in outputs/<name>/: demo.mp4, stills/, and proof-log.md saying exactly what happened, step by step.

Good to know

  • Two kinds of take. A demo proves how something works — a client walkthrough, a clip for a pull request, a lesson — and keeps everything but your product out of the frame. That is the default. A launch video presents your product in public, and only then do a title card, music and a closing card belong. Your agent asks where it is going; if you shrug, it makes a demo.
  • Retake never starts your app without you saying so.
  • Apps behind a login: when the agent needs a sign-in, a form appears here asking for a demo account. What you type is written to one local file (.env in your workspace) and never sent to the agent or anywhere else. The sign-in runs before the camera and is cut off; the session is kept, so later takes skip it. For codes from your phone, SSO or a captcha: retake signin demos/<name>.yaml — log in once by hand, Retake keeps the session.
  • A failed step names what was on screen at the time — read the proof log before guessing.
