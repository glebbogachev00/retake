## One sentence becomes two places
**Story:** Type “fix the signup bug before Friday and I keep going back and forth on usage-based pricing vs seats,” press Capture, then show the new Action and Thread on the board.  
**Length:** ~25s  
**Why it lands:** A viewer sees one rough thought become an action with a shelf life and a thread that can grow.

## Close the loop
**Story:** Capture “send Maya the revised contract today,” open the resulting Action, then press Mark done and show it fade from the open list.  
**Length:** ~25s  
**Why it lands:** A viewer understands that actions are meant to be closed, not merely collected.

## Give an action a shelf life
**Story:** Capture “renew the domain,” open its Action, use Change shelf life to set when it matters, then return to the board with the dated action visible.  
**Length:** ~25s  
**Why it lands:** A viewer sees that an action carries urgency instead of becoming an undifferentiated task.

## Let a question become a thread
**Story:** Capture “what should our onboarding promise be,” open the created Thread, add a Fragment, and show the thread with both pieces of thinking.  
**Length:** ~25s  
**Why it lands:** A viewer understands that unresolved questions have a place to accumulate context.

## Search the thought, not the filing system
**Story:** Capture a note about “pricing,” use Search everything for “pricing,” and open the matching Thread from the results.  
**Length:** ~25s  
**Why it lands:** A viewer sees they can retrieve a thought from its words without deciding where to file it first.

## Distill a messy note
**Story:** Enter a rambling thought in the capture field, choose Distill instead of capture, then show the distilled wording before accepting it onto the board.  
**Length:** ~25s  
**Why it lands:** A viewer understands they can speak or type naturally and refine the wording afterwards.
