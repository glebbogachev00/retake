How it works

You provide intent. The agent writes the flow. Retake verifies, records, and renders it.

Tell your agent what to show

"Record a demo of checkout." That sentence is the whole brief.

It builds and checks the flow

The agent reads your app — routes, selectors, sign-in fields, unstable UI states — writes the demo as a small readable file, and dry-runs every step in seconds. Nothing records until the walkthrough actually works.

Retake records the real interaction

Real typing, real clicks, real waits — then hands back demo.mp4, a still per scene, and a proof log of what happened, step by step.

Rerun or re-render when something changes

UI moved? Change one line, run again. Want it calmer, square, captionless? Say so — most changes re-render in seconds, no browser involved.

What people record

One engine, different audiences. The demo is a file, so each of these is a re-run — not an afternoon.

Client walkthroughs

Show the flow they asked about, at their pace, without booking a call. When they ask "can it also…", edit two lines and send a fresh take.

Launches and social

A square or vertical render of a real interaction beats a screenshot with arrows. Ship a feature, post the 20 seconds that show it.

Docs and onboarding

The README GIF, the first-run walkthrough, the help-page clip — regenerated when the UI changes, so they never quietly rot.

Proof it still works

The dry run walks every step of every demo in seconds — a demo that fails when your app breaks is a regression test you can also watch, and a repro you can attach to the ticket.

What's in the box

Everything required to produce the same clean take again.

Scenes with captions

Captions land on the frames they describe, timed from the actual run — never burned into the product area.

Render-time camera

Zoom and framing applied after recording. Change your mind in seconds, no browser involved.

Presets that post well

1080p landscape, square, vertical, docs GIF — think in the publishing format, not the browser window.

A still for every scene

Each scene yields a crisp PNG. A video usually ships with a picture next to it.

Secrets stay secret

Credentials live in .env, redacted from logs, warned about if they'd appear on camera.

Stubs and seeds

Fake the backend, reset the state — demos stay repeatable even when the world isn't.
