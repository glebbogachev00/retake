03 / A few practical answers

Before you
press record.

Is this an AI video generator?+

Retake records a real web app in a browser. Your coding agent plans and runs the walkthrough; the footage comes from the app, not generated scenes.

Can I add voiceover and captions?+

Yes. Choose voiceover, captions, both, or neither when your agent makes the video. Start with a silent preview, then review the take and narration script before adding voiceover.

What can I record?+

Browser-based product videos, tutorials, content clips, and walkthroughs. Retake drives Chromium with Playwright. It is not a general desktop or native mobile screen recorder.

Do I need a Retake account or API key?+

No Retake account or Retake API key is needed for local recording. You need Node.js 20 or newer and a coding agent or the CLI. Setup downloads Chromium. Optional model features may need their own provider configuration.

Does anything leave my machine?+

The recording and local review library run on your machine. Your coding agent may use its own model provider. Optional visual checks send frames to the model you configure, and some narration options use external services. Review those settings before recording sensitive workflows.

Will the video update automatically?+

The flow is reusable, not self-healing. A routine can rerun it on a schedule on your Mac, or ask your agent to rerun it after a change. If buttons, selectors, or the workflow have changed, it may need to update the steps first. Want it to run without your Mac being on? Tell me.

Is Retake free?+

Retake is open source under the MIT license. Recording uses your computer. Your coding agent and any optional model or narration services may have their own costs. Rather not set it up yourself? I can make the videos for you: email me.
