How it works

Retake is a recording engine your coding agent drives. You talk; it does the careful part.

It reads your app

Routes, selectors, sign-in fields, the things that animate forever — from the live page and the source folder.

It writes the demo as a small file

Steps, scenes, captions — YAML you can read. That file is the demo; it lives in your repo like code.

It checks before it records

A dry run walks every selector and wait in seconds, with no camera. Nothing records until the walkthrough actually works.

You get the take, and the receipts

demo.mp4, one still per scene, and a proof log of what happened, step by step, with timings.

You direct it in plain words

“No zooms.” “Hide the cursor.” “Square for a post.” Said once, remembered per project. Most changes re-render in seconds.

What's in the box

The boring, verifiable middle of demo-making — so neither you nor your agent has to hold it.

Real interactions

Real typing, real scrolls, real waits on real results — not a slideshow with a cursor drawn on top.

Scenes with captions

Captions land on the frames they describe, timed from the actual run — never burned into the product area.

Render-time camera

Zoom and framing applied after recording. Change your mind in seconds, no browser involved.

Presets that post well

1080p landscape, square, vertical, docs GIF — think in the publishing format, not the browser window.

Stills for free

Every scene yields a crisp PNG. A video usually ships with a picture next to it.

Secrets stay secret

Credentials live in .env, redacted from logs, warned about if they'd appear on camera.

Stubs and seeds

Fake the backend, reset the state — demos stay repeatable even when the world isn't.

A review window

Watch your agent work live, judge the take, archive what's stale. One page, no chat to babysit.

Two doors, same engine

Use the agent you already pay for — or the window, if you'd rather point and click.
