Testing you can watch.

Your agent runs your app’s key flows in a real browser. Watch it live or replay it, and say where something looks unclear.

  • Live, or as a replay. Each step at your pace, or the finished session later.
  • Your notes become fixes. Press Unclear, say why, and the note goes to your agent.
  • Checked after deploys, and every night. Silent unless something changed.

03 / A few practical answers

Before you
press record.

Is this an AI video generator?+

Retake records a real web app in a browser. Your coding agent plans and runs the walkthrough; the footage comes from the app, not generated scenes.

Can I add voiceover and captions?+

Yes. Choose voiceover, captions, both, or neither when your agent makes the video. Start with a silent preview, then review the take and narration script before adding voiceover.

What can I record?+

Anything that runs as a website or web app: product videos, tutorials, content clips and walkthroughs. Retake drives Chromium, a browser, with Playwright. On a Mac it can also record one Mac app’s window, after a one-time setup it walks you through. It is not a general desktop or native mobile screen recorder.

Do I need a Retake account or API key?+

No Retake account or Retake API key is needed for local recording. You need Node.js 20 or newer, and Claude Code or Codex signed in for the Retake window's first draft; other coding agents and the CLI work too. Setup downloads Chromium. Optional model features may need their own provider configuration.

Does anything leave my machine?+

The recording and local review library run on your machine. Your coding agent may use its own model provider. Optional visual checks send frames to the model you configure, and some narration options use external services. Review those settings before recording sensitive workflows.

Will the video update automatically?+

The flow is reusable, not self-healing. Ask your agent to rerun it after a change. If buttons, selectors, or the workflow have changed, it may need to update the steps first.

Is Retake free?+

Retake is open source under the MIT license. Recording uses your computer. Your coding agent and any optional model or narration services may have their own costs.
